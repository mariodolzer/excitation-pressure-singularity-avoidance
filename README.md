# Excitation Pressure and Singularity Avoidance

Independent research project by **Mario Dolzer** on the role of excitation pressure \(P(x,t)\) in semiclassical gravity and its implications for the avoidance of true gravitational singularities in black holes.

## Abstract (Summary)

The excitation pressure framework \(P(x,t)\) describes the renormalized interaction energy density of quantum fields as a spatially distributed source term in the semiclassical Einstein equations. In both works it is shown that:

- the renormalized stress-energy tensor \(\langle T_{\mu\nu} \rangle_{\text{ren}}\) remains smooth and finite under Hadamard renormalization,  
- point-like sources are replaced by extended interaction "clouds" with a minimal effective size,  
- classical energy conditions (NEC, SEC) can be violated by vacuum polarization and Casimir-like configurations,  
- this allows defocusing in the Raychaudhuri equation and weakens the premises of classical Penrose-Hawking singularity theorems,  
- stochastic metric fluctuations in the Einstein-Langevin framework replace a sharp classical singularity by a region of extreme but finite interaction energy.

The conclusion is that excitation pressure provides a conservative, mathematically consistent and plausible path towards the avoidance of true singularities in semiclassical gravity, but does not yet constitute a complete proof. Open problems include fully dynamical collapse simulations, curvature invariant calculations, the treatment of large fluctuations and the integration of genuine quantum gravity effects.
